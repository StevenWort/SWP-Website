+ Steel Blue Accent — the coolest, most modern option. Echoes blade steel and mirrors. Best if you want the deck to feel sleek/editorial rather than warm/traditional.

My pick: Option B typography (Playfair Display italic + Bebas Neue eyebrow) with the oxblood accent — it keeps your dark‑grey base intact, nods to barbershop heritage in one deliberate color rather than a literal red/white/blue palette, and the serif/condensed‑sans mix reads as editorial rather than generic template. Brass is the close second if you want something a touch more "luxury barbering" instead of "vintage."
