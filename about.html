About

I am a Photographer based in the Pacific North West. I offer a wide range of services including portrait, event, and commercial photography. My approach is simple: honest light, genuine moments, and images that still mean something years from now.

When I'm not behind the camera, I'm usually scouting new locations or poring over the last shoot's contact sheets looking for the one frame that got it just right.

Artist Statement

I've always been drawn to the idea of capturing moments in time — the memories, emotions, and small details that define our lives. I started with film, shooting hundreds of rolls filled with family snapshots, holidays, and social gatherings, always chasing that elusive image that brings us back to a specific moment. My goal has never been just to document what happened, but to create photographs that evoke the feeling of being there. A meaningful image should carry the emotion, the smell, the taste, and the sounds of that moment, allowing us to step back into it long after the moment has passed.

My work is rooted in a deep sense of place, using atmosphere, architecture, and natural light to reveal emotional truth. I create pictures that feel intentional, quiet, and grounded, inviting viewers into the subtle stories held within each environment.
